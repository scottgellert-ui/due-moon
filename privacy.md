# Due Moon privacy policy

Due Moon does not collect, store or share any personal information.

- The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no purchases.
- Your progress is saved only on your own device.
- The game does not connect to the internet while you play.

If you have a question, open an issue at https://github.com/scottgellert-ui/due-moon/issues.

Last updated: 11 November 2026.
