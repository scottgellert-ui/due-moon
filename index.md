# Due Moon support

Stuck on Sevens? Here's how to get help.

**Report a problem or ask a question:** open an issue at https://github.com/scottgellert-ui/due-moon/issues and describe what happened. A screenshot helps.

## Common questions

**Is there any betting or real money in Due Moon?**
No. Due Moon is a story-driven card game based on 21. There is no betting or wagering of any kind, no real money, nothing to buy and no prizes. Your debt to Mr. Vig is counted in tally marks that only exist inside the game, and winning hands crosses them off.

**Where is my progress saved?**
On your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

**The music stopped.**
Open the menu and check the music volume, and make sure your iPhone isn't on silent.

**I ran out of hands before making the night's quota.**
That's the end of the night, and Mr. Vig charges a late fee. At Rock Bottom you can cover it (sweep the floor, sing karaoke, ask a favour, Pip's stash or Mr Teeth) or just pay it, then a fresh night starts.

**Can I make the text bigger or change the suit colours?**
Yes. Open the menu for bigger text, colour-blind suits and fewer screen flashes.

## Privacy

Due Moon collects no personal information. Read the [privacy policy](privacy).
