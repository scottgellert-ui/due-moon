# Due Moon support

Stuck on Sevens? Here's how to get help.

**Report a problem or ask a question:** open an issue at https://github.com/scottgellert-ui/due-moon/issues and describe what happened. A screenshot helps.

## Common questions

**Is there real money in Due Moon?**
No. Due Moon is a game of simulated gambling for entertainment. There is no real money, nothing to buy and no prizes.

**Where is my progress saved?**
On your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

**The music stopped.**
Open the menu and check the music volume, and make sure your iPhone isn't on silent.

**I'm completely broke and stuck.**
When you have no chips, Rock Bottom offers ways back. Look for Pip's stash, a favour, a gadget or Mr Teeth.

**Can I make the text bigger or change the suit colours?**
Yes. Open the menu for bigger text, colour-blind suits and fewer screen flashes.

## Privacy

Due Moon collects no personal information. Read the [privacy policy](privacy).
